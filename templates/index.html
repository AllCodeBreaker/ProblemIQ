About

Problem IQ is a lightweight learning and practice platform focused on aptitude patterns commonly asked in interviews and competitive exams. It organizes content into clear topics, offers short lessons, and provides practice problems to help you apply concepts.

Features:

  • Curated topic lists
  • Practice exercises (coming soon)
  • Open-source content and community contributions
